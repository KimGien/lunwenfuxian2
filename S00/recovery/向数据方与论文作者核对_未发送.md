# 原始数据与 TRI-DEP 缺失处理核对稿（未发送）

目标：向 MODMA 数据保管方核对完整录音，向 TRI-DEP 作者确认实际实验政策。不要直接发布原始录音到公开 issue。

## 给 MODMA 数据方

Subject: MODMA audio integrity query: subject 02010004, recordings 23–28

Dear MODMA dataset maintainers,

We are preparing a reproduction of TRI-DEP (arXiv:2510.14922v2) using MODMA. In the copies currently available to us, subject 02010004 has the following audio integrity problems:

- Recordings 24.wav through 28.wav have no valid RIFF/WAVE header and cannot be decoded by Python wave or FFmpeg.
- Recording 23.wav is decodable, but its tail from approximately 11 seconds shows severe noise: RMS approximately 0.577, about 5% of samples above 0.95 absolute amplitude, and near-zero lag-one correlation.
- A separately obtained public mirror contains byte-identical copies of all six files. An alternate EEGLAB export omits recordings 24–28; its recording 23 PCM exactly matches the problematic WAV.

A 2021 study (DOI: 10.3390/ijerph18126441, Section 2.4) also reports defective audio for this subject. Could you confirm whether corrected original recordings 23–28 are available, and direct us to the appropriate authorized access procedure? If these files are irrecoverable, an official clarification of the affected recordings would also be useful.

We can provide SHA-256 values and a compact diagnostic report. Thank you.

## 给 TRI-DEP 作者

Subject: Reproducing TRI-DEP v2: handling damaged MODMA recordings for 02010004

Dear TRI-DEP authors,

We are preparing a reproduction of arXiv:2510.14922v2 using the published subject-level fold_assignments.json. Subject 02010004 is included in that cohort, but our available MODMA audio copies have unreadable recordings 24–28 and a severe-noise tail in recording 23. A 2021 MODMA study (DOI: 10.3390/ijerph18126441) independently mentions defective audio for this subject.

Could you clarify how these files were handled in the experiments reported in v2?

1. Did you have intact original recordings 23–28, or were any recordings excluded/replaced?
2. If recordings were unavailable, were their speech vectors zero-filled? How were the corresponding text inputs represented, and was recording 23's noisy tail retained?
3. Does the development branch's zero-vector fallback for missing speech segments and `[ERROR]` ASR placeholder reproduce the actual experimental handling?

Our public-mirror hashes and a read-only diagnostic report are available for comparison. We would appreciate guidance on obtaining the same input data or faithfully documenting the missing-data policy. Thank you.
